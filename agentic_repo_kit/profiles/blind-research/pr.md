### Blind-research provenance

- [ ] The active blind-research gate/boundary is defined by durable project-local policy or roadmap state rather than invented in this PR.
- [ ] Target-specific findings disclose provenance as `clean`, `contaminated`, or `external-assisted` (or the project's explicit equivalent) separately from evidence class.
- [ ] Accidental target-specific disclosure is recorded and potentially influenced conclusions remain contaminated even after independent correctness corroboration.
- [ ] Any pre-unlock external rescue links a prior durable blocker/negative result and a bounded maintainer unlock; dependent findings remain external-assisted.
- [ ] Post-blind external comparison preserves the independent result and reports agreements/disagreements separately rather than rewriting the blind record.
- [ ] Unsupported repository history or operator-provided target-specific recovered artifacts were not used as an undeclared shortcut.
