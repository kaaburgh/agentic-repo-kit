## Project blind-research gate

For this example project, the blind-research gate is active until milestone M1 is `Completed and verified` in `ROADMAP.md`.

While the gate is active, target-specific recovered knowledge outside the supported repository state is excluded from normal research. The supported repository state is current `main` plus the branch/PR under review.

A pre-M1 rescue requires both:

1. a concrete blocker or negative result recorded under `docs/experiments/`; and
2. a dated decision in the relevant `ROADMAP.md` item naming the bounded question and allowed external source class and stating that the exception does not generalize.

Findings depending on such a rescue are `external-assisted`. After M1, external target-specific research may be used only as a separately recorded verification/corroboration phase after the independent result is preserved.
